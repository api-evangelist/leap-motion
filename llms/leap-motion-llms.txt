# Leap Motion (Ultraleap)

> Leap Motion is the hand-tracking company behind the Leap Motion Controller. It merged with Ultrahaptics in 2019 to form Ultraleap; the Leap Motion brand continues as Ultraleap's hardware line (Leap Motion Controller 2) and the "Leap" naming across the tracking SDK. The developer surface is not a hosted web API — it is LeapC, a native C API that connects a local client to the Ultraleap Hand Tracking Service and delivers tracked hands, digits, bones, images and device events as polled messages, plus first-party plugins for Unity, Unreal, OpenXR and Python.

## APIs
- [LeapC Tracking API](https://docs.ultraleap.com/api-reference/tracking-api/index.html): Native C API; open a LEAP_CONNECTION to the local Hand Tracking Service and drive a poll loop for tracking/device/policy/config/image events.
- [Ultraleap Tracking WebSocket](https://github.com/ultraleap/UltraleapTrackingWebSocket): Open-source loopback WebSocket restoring the legacy LeapJS "v6" JSON protocol for browsers.
